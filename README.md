# Bible & World Timeline

An interactive timeline from creation to AD 100. It places the people and events of the Bible alongside the empires, rulers and thinkers of the ancient world, so you can see who was alive at the same time.

## What's in it

- **210 entries on eight tracks:** people of the Bible, kings of Judah, kings of Israel, prophets, Bible events, empires and nations, world rulers, and the wider world
- **Tap any entry** to read about it, with Bible references, and see everyone alive or in power at the same time
- **Era shortcuts** from Creation to Abraham through to Jesus and the apostles
- **Search** for any person or event
- **14 cards** on places where the Bible meets other ancient records
- **Full screen** (button or the F key) and a **light / dark / auto** colour switch
- **Works on phones:** swipe to travel, pinch to zoom, tap for details

## How to open it

It is a single file with no setup. Open `index.html` in any web browser.

## About the dates

- **Genesis 1–11** is dated by adding up the ages in Genesis 5 and 11 (Hebrew text). That puts creation at about 4174 BC and the Flood at about 2518 BC. These entries have dashed outlines because scholars do not agree on these dates.
- **From Abraham onward**, the timeline follows a traditional chronology: the Exodus in 1446 BC (1 Kings 6:1) and Edwin Thiele's dates for the kings.
- **Egypt and Mesopotamia** follow the standard historical chronology.

Anything before about 900 BC is approximate. The page itself explains where views differ.
